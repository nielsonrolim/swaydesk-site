Swaydesk turns [Sway](https://swaywm.org/) into a complete desktop without losing what makes it good: it is light, fast and fully driven by the keyboard when you want it. Swaydesk is an independent project: it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Sway project or its developers.

On top of Sway come the pieces a desktop needs: a bar with network, Bluetooth, audio, battery, brightness and calendar popups; a dock with your pinned and open apps; an app grid; a notification center; clipboard history; a lock screen; a login screen; Alt+Tab window switching; screenshots and screen recording; and an app to pick the theme and the accent color. The components are its own programs, written in Rust with GTK 4.

## What you get

- **Shortcuts you already know:** the Swaydesk scheme by default, inspired by KDE Plasma with Krohnkite, or the i3, Hyprland, niri or dwm keys. See the [shortcuts](/en-us/shortcuts/).
- **Effortless tiling:** windows arrange themselves, and you can still float a window, make it fullscreen or put it aside.
- **One look:** the theme, the icons and the accent color change together in the bar, the dock, the popups and in GTK and libadwaita apps.
- **In your language:** English, Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish, in the desktop and in the installer.
- **No surprises:** the session has a password agent, a keyring, a portal for screen sharing and update notices, like the bigger desktops.
