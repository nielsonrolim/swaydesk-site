The packages for Debian 13, Ubuntu 26.04 and Arch Linux will come from a repository on the [Open Build Service](https://build.opensuse.org/). As soon as it passes the tests, the full instructions will be here.

## What you will need

- Debian 13, Ubuntu 26.04 or Arch Linux, with or without another desktop. The desktop you already have stays on the login screen.
- An Intel, AMD or NVIDIA graphics card with a Wayland driver.
